One-time language download

Click the green button. Chrome will download this language's local speech model, then OWL-Note returns you to the video and starts transcribing. Nothing else is needed — leave this tab open until it finishes.

What happens next?

  • Chrome installs the selected language resource once, for every later session.
  • Speech is recognized on your device by Chrome's native engine.
  • Audio is captured only from the tab you started, and never uploaded or stored.
  • OWL-Note never uses cloud transcription.
