Killed enough plants to finally write down what each one actually wants. Watering
is where I always go wrong — more of them die from love than neglect.

- **Monstera:** only water when the top inch of soil is dry. Bright indirect
  light. Wipe the leaves, it hates dust.
- **Snake plant:** basically ignore it. A drink every three weeks, less in
  winter. Will rot instantly if you're generous.
- **Fiddle leaf fig:** the diva. Hates being moved, hates drafts, wants a
  consistent bright spot and a routine. Do not relocate it "for a change of
  scenery."
- **Pothos:** the forgiving one. Tolerates low light, tells you when it's thirsty
  by drooping dramatically, perks right back up.

General rule I keep forgetting: finger in the soil beats any watering schedule.
