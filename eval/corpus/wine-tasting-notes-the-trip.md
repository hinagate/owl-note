Long day out in the hills, three wineries, and I am writing this slightly
tipsy so forgive the handwriting-of-the-soul.

The standout, no contest, was the **Nebbiolo** at the second place — all firm
tannin and dried cherry and something almost like roses underneath. Bought two
bottles, which felt restrained given how much I wanted the case.

The others: a crisp white at the first stop, forgettable in a pleasant way; and a
big jammy red at the third that everyone but me loved. Our guide, **Luca**, was
wonderful — knew every vine's life story and poured with a generous hand.

Note for future me: the good stuff is worth the detour to the small family places,
not the big tour-bus one. And eat more than one breadstick before an 11 a.m.
tasting.
