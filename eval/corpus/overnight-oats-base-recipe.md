The lazy breakfast. Assemble at night, eat straight from the jar in the morning
like a raccoon in a hurry.

Base (one jar):
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk (any kind; oat milk makes it extra creamy)
- 1 tbsp chia seeds — these do the thickening
- 1 tsp maple syrup, pinch of salt

Stir it all in the jar, lid on, refrigerate **overnight** — about **8 hours** is
the sweet spot, that's when the chia has fully set and the oats have softened. In
the morning it should be spoonable, not soup; add a splash of milk if it's too
stiff.

Toppings are the whole personality: berries, banana, peanut butter, toasted nuts.
Keeps three days made, so I batch a few jars on Sunday.
