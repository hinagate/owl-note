title: "Weeknight red lentil dahl"
lang: en
tags: [recipe]
---

Fast, cheap, one pot. Serves 3, or 2 with leftovers for lunch.

Ingredients:
- 1 cup red lentils, rinsed
- 400 ml tin of coconut milk
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic + thumb of ginger
- 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- a knob of ghee

Method: temper the cumin seeds in the ghee until they pop, then soften the onion,
garlic and ginger. Stir in the ground spices for a minute. Add the lentils, the
coconut milk and about a mug of water, bring to a simmer and cook for 25 minutes,
stirring now and then so it doesn't catch. Finish with lemon and salt.

It thickens a lot as it sits — loosen leftovers with a splash of water when you
reheat. Good with rice, better with a warm flatbread.
