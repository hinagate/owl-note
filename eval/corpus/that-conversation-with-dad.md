---
title: "That conversation with Dad"
lang: en
tags: [journal]
---

Called Dad tonight and somewhere between the weather and the football he told me
he's **selling the lake cabin**.

Sat with that for a second. It's been in the family my whole life — the wobbly
dock, the smell of the boathouse, learning to swim off the end of it. But he said
the upkeep's gotten to be too much, the roof needs doing again, and honestly he'd
rather spend the money and the years he's got left **travelling** than fixing a
place he gets to twice a summer.

And he's right. I know he's right. Doesn't make it not sting. I asked if I could
come up one last weekend before it lists and he said of course, bring the good
coffee. So we'll do that. Bittersweet is the exact word and I hate that it's the
right one.
