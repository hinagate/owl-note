title: "Moving apartments — the good and the dread"

---

Signed the lease on the Elm Street place today. It's happening.

The good: there's a **balcony**, actual morning light, and the kitchen has more
than one usable drawer. Move date is **14 June**, which gives me five weeks to
pretend I'll pack in an orderly fashion instead of in one screaming weekend.

The dread: the rent's a fair bit higher, and I hate moving with a specific,
bone-deep hatred. Boxes, tape, the guilt of everything I own. And telling this
landlord I'm leaving.

But honestly — at least the new place won't have that **radiator** hissing and
dripping all night. I will not miss standing on a cold floor at 2 a.m. moving a
saucepan. Small mercies. Onward.
