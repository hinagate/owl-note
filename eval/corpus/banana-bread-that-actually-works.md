title: "Banana bread that actually works"
lang: en
tags: [recipe]

After three sad, sunken loaves, this is the one. The trick was browning the
butter and not overmixing.

- 3 very ripe bananas (black-spotted, the uglier the better), mashed
- 115 g butter, browned and cooled slightly
- 150 g brown sugar
- 2 eggs
- 180 g plain flour
- 1 tsp baking soda, pinch of salt

Mash the bananas, whisk in the browned butter, sugar and eggs, then fold in the
flour and soda *just* until no streaks remain — lumps are fine, a smooth batter
means tough bread. Into a lined loaf tin.

Bake at **175°C for 55 minutes**, until a skewer comes out with a few moist
crumbs. If the top browns too fast, tent it with foil at the 40 minute mark. Cool
in the tin for ten minutes before turning out, or it tears.
