Grandma's caramel, from the index card, transcribed before the card falls apart.

- 200 g white sugar
- 120 ml double cream, warmed
- 40 g butter
- a real pinch of salt

Melt the sugar dry in a heavy pan, swirling (don't stir) until it's a deep amber.
Cook it to the **soft-ball stage, 240°F** on the thermometer — that's the number
Grandma underlined twice. Take it off the heat, then whisk in the warm cream a
splash at a time; it will hiss and climb the pan, that's normal. Beat in the
butter and the salt.
