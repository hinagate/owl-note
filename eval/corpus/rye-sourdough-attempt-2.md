# Rye sourdough — attempt 2

Second go at the rye loaf. Wrote down everything this time so I can actually
compare.

- **Flour:** 20% rye, 80% bread flour
- **Hydration:** 72%
- **Starter:** 100 g, fed the night before
- **Bulk ferment:** 3.5 hours at room temp (about 21°C in the kitchen)
- **Bake:** 230°C for 40 minutes, first 20 with the lid on the Dutch oven

Crumb came out a little dense and gummy in the middle — I think I underbaked it
and the hydration was on the low side for rye, which drinks a lot of water. The
crust was good though, proper crackle when it cooled.

Next time: push the hydration up and the oven hotter. Also let the bulk go longer,
the dough didn't look as puffy as I wanted before shaping.
