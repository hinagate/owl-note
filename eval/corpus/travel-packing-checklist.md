The list I forget every single time until I'm at the gate. Carry-on only, always.

Essentials (the "can't buy at destination easily" tier):
- passport + a photo of it on my phone
- universal adapter (Type C and G both, learned that the hard way)
- melatonin for the flight, the good earplugs
- refillable water bottle, empty through security
- the noise-cancelling headphones, not the cheap pair

Meh, buy there if forgotten: toothpaste, sunscreen, a phone charger.

Golden rules: roll don't fold, wear the bulkiest shoes on the plane, and one
outfit fewer than I think I need. I have never once run out of clothes. I have
often run out of bag.
