# Training log — week 16

Peak week, and I'm still standing, which after sixteen weeks feels like the real
victory.

- **Long run:** hit **32 km** on Sunday, the longest of the whole block. Legs
  were gone by the end but I ran the whole thing, no walk breaks.
- Weekly mileage landed at about **55 km**, my highest.
- One tempo session, one easy recovery jog, rest deliberately lazy.

**Taper starts next week** — this is the scary part, the "am I doing enough"
itch, but the training's in the bank now and you can't cram fitness. The race is
in **three weeks**.

Body check: left hamstring a little tight, foam-rolling it nightly. Sleep and food
are the whole job from here. Trust the plan, don't do anything heroic.
