# HOA meeting — 15 April

Turnout was better than usual, maybe fifteen units represented.

Decisions (voted):
- **Repaint the lobby** — approved, budget capped at **$3,200**. The greige won
  over the blue, 7 to 2.
- New **bike rack** by the side entrance — approved. Was overdue, the current one
  fits four bikes and there are clearly a dozen.
- **Quiet hours** moved earlier, now **10 pm to 7 am** (was 11 pm).

Open items:
- The leak in the parking garage — still getting quotes, deferred to May.
- Someone raised the recycling situation again; agreed to add a second bin.

Next meeting third Tuesday of May.
