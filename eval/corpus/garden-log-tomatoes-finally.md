---
title: "Garden log: tomatoes finally"
lang: en
tags: [journal]
---

The **San Marzano** tomatoes I put in back in April are finally turning. Picked
the first three properly ripe ones this morning, still warm from the sun, and ate
one standing right there in the dirt like an animal. Worth every aphid.

Less good news from the rest of the plot:
- The basil bolted the second it got hot. Flowers everywhere, leaves gone bitter.
  Lesson: pinch it back harder, earlier.
- Squirrels got two of the ripening tomatoes. One clean bite each, the little
  editors. Netting is going up this weekend.

The zucchini, as ever, is threatening to take over the county. Otherwise a good
morning out there. Dirt under the nails is good for the soul.
