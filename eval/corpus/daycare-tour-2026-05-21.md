# Sunnybrook daycare tour — 21 May 2026

Toured the place, took notes while it was fresh.

- **Ratio:** one caregiver to **four toddlers** in the room we'd be in. Felt calm,
  not chaotic.
- **Waitlist:** about **6 months**, so we'd need to decide and put the deposit
  down basically now to line up with going back to work.
- **Cost:** **$1,250 a month**, which is roughly what we'd feared but not worse.
- Meals included, they cook on site, and the outdoor space is genuinely nice.

Vibe was warm, the staff had clearly been there years (good sign), and it smelled
like crayons and soup rather than bleach.

**Decision:** join the waitlist. Put the deposit down this week.
