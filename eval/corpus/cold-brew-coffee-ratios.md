title: "Cold brew coffee ratios"
lang: en

Dialled in the cold brew so I'm not eyeballing it every time.

- Ratio: **1:8** coffee to water by weight. My jar takes 100 g coffee to 800 g
  water.
- Grind: coarse, like breadcrumbs. Anything finer and it goes muddy and bitter.
- Steep: **16 hours** in the fridge. I set it up before bed and strain it after
  work the next day.
- Strain through a paper filter into a clean jar. Keeps about a week.

Serve it diluted 1:1 with water or milk over ice — the concentrate on its own is
intense. If it tastes sour it was under-extracted (grind slightly finer or steep
longer); if it's harsh and dry it over-extracted (coarser, or shorter steep).
