---
title: "Rainy Tuesday and the leaking radiator"
lang: en
tags: [journal]
---

Grey all day, the kind of rain that never commits to actually pouring. I liked it.
Made a pot of tea and got more done before noon than I usually manage in a week.

Then the bedroom radiator decided to start dripping. Not a burst, just this slow
tick of water pooling on the floor behind it. Shoved a towel down and a saucepan
under the valve to catch it, which is very much a "future problem" solution.
Messaged the landlord — he says the plumber can come **Thursday**. So two nights
of listening to it drip.

Weirdly not that annoyed about it. The tea helped. Ordered too much Thai food and
watched something forgettable and that was the evening. Some Tuesdays are just
maintenance.
