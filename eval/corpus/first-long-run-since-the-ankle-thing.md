title: "First long run since the ankle thing"
lang: en
tags: [journal]
---

Did it. First proper distance since I rolled the ankle back in January and spent
six weeks feeling sorry for myself.

Went out slow on purpose, no watch anxiety, just easy. Ended up covering **8 km**
before I even thought about the ankle — and it held. No twinge, no swelling after.
Pace was somewhere around 6:30 a kilometre which is glacial for me but I genuinely
did not care.

The last stretch along the canal I had that stupid grin going. Forgot how much I
missed this. Ice on the ankle tonight as a precaution, and I'm not going to get
greedy and double the distance on Thursday. Slow build. But I'm back.
