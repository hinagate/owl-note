title: "Sichuan mapo tofu (my version)"
lang: en
tags: [recipe]
---

The numbing-spicy one. Don't skip the peppercorn, that's the whole point.

- 300 g soft (silken) tofu, cut into 2 cm cubes, held in warm salted water
- 150 g ground pork
- 1 tbsp doubanjiang (fermented broad bean paste) — this is the flavour base
- 1 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorn — the tingly, mouth-numbing kick
- 2 cloves garlic, spring onion, a little chilli oil
- cornstarch slurry to finish

Brown the pork hard, push it aside and fry the doubanjiang in the fat until the
oil turns red. Add garlic, a splash of stock, then slide in the tofu and simmer
gently so it doesn't break. Thicken with the slurry in two additions. Off the
heat, shower over the ground Sichuan peppercorn and the green tops of the spring
onion.

Eat it with plain rice. The contrast is the whole dish — bland rice, loud tofu.
