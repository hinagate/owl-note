# Rye sourdough — attempt 3

Third go at the rye loaf. Took the notes from last time and pushed everything I
said I would.

- **Flour:** 25% rye, 75% bread flour
- **Hydration:** 78%
- **Starter:** 100 g, fed the night before
- **Bulk ferment:** 4 hours at room temp (about 21°C in the kitchen)
- **Bake:** 245°C for 35 minutes, first 20 with the lid on the Dutch oven

Much better oven spring — the wetter dough and the hotter oven made a real
difference. Crumb was open and springy instead of gummy, and the extra rye gave
it a deeper, almost malty flavour. Slightly darker crust from the higher heat but
not burnt.

This is the one to repeat. Only tweak for next time: score a bit deeper so it
opens along the cut instead of bursting at the side.
